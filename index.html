Stefan J. Dietrich

PhD Candidate in Economics · Boston University

Fields: Economic History · Innovation · Applied Microeconomics

I work in economic history, with interests in innovation, industrialization, and long-run growth. My research builds new historical microdata to ask how institutions and professional networks shaped technological change.

Job Market Paper

Engineering Germany’s Industrial Success: Engineering Societies, Patents, and the 19th-Century Catch-Up

I ask whether organizing practical knowledge speeds up industrialization. During Germany’s industrial revolution, regions where engineers formed local professional societies pulled ahead in innovation — and the advantage compounded, leaving these regions more industrialized well into the twentieth century.
